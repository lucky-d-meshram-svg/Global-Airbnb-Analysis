# 🌍 Global Airbnb Performance Dashboards

This project presents a series of interactive dashboards that analyze Airbnb’s global performance metrics across major cities. The visualizations combine insights on market share, pricing, reviews, seasonality, trust, and guest satisfaction, offering a comprehensive overview of Airbnb’s worldwide activity.

## 📊 Key Features

### Market Share by City
- Paris, New York, and Sydney lead in listings.  
- Superhost participation varies across markets.  
- Cumulative percentage lines highlight global supply concentration.  

### Pricing Analysis
- Hotel rooms average the highest price ($800).  
- Private rooms are the most affordable ($462).  
- Entire places and shared rooms fall in between, showing Airbnb’s flexibility across budgets.  

### Review Frequency & Seasonality
- 86% of guests leave only one review, with nearly 100% cumulative by six reviews.  
- Seasonal demand differs by city: Paris and Rome peak in summer, Sydney in December–January, while New York and Mexico City show steadier year-round engagement.  

### Trust & Verification
- 66.9% of hosts are fully verified.  
- Only 0.3% remain unverified, while nearly all others provide at least one trust signal.  
- Demonstrates Airbnb’s emphasis on safety and credibility.  

### Guest Ratings & Satisfaction
- **Top-rated cities:** Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro (~9.8–9.9).  
- **Lowest-rated cities:** Hong Kong and Istanbul (~9.0–9.1).  
- Cleanliness and value for money are the most challenging metrics globally.  
- Overall averages remain high (9.3–9.7), reflecting strong guest satisfaction worldwide.  

## 🎯 Purpose
These dashboards are designed to:
- Provide data-driven insights for Airbnb’s global performance.  
- Highlight seasonal demand patterns and pricing strategies.  
- Support business decision-making with clear, comparative visuals.  

---

✨ **In summary:** This project showcases Airbnb’s strengths in global reach, host verification, and guest satisfaction, while identifying improvement areas such as cleanliness and value consistency.
